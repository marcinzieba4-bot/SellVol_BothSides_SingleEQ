TradFI Strategy

InsideSPX Momentum

Each month, screen the top-100 S&P 500 members and buy 1-month at-the-money calls on every member whose prior-month return was positive. Call premiums tie up only ~1.3% of capital on average; the rest sits in cash earning the effective Fed Funds rate. The strategy exploits short-term momentum through the most efficient part of the vol surface — near-the-money short-dated calls where skew is minimal and liquidity is deep.

How It Works

Four mechanical steps, run at the close of every month with no discretion.

01
Universe selection

Top-100 SPX Members

From the S&P 500, only the largest 100 members by market cap are eligible — updated year by year to reflect the actual index composition at each point in time. This keeps the universe liquid, optionable, and free of backfill bias.

02
Signal

1-Month Price Momentum

Any member whose prior-month return was positive receives a buy signal. Equal weight is applied across all signal-positive names each month — typically ~60 concurrent positions (range 2–94), providing broad diversification within the momentum cohort.

03
Instrument choice

ATM Calls — Best of the Vol Curve

Rather than holding stock, the strategy buys 1-month at-the-money calls on each name. Near-the-money options sit at the sweetest point of the volatility surface: skew is minimal (unlike OTM puts, which carry a large premium), bid-ask spreads are tightest, and the instrument delivers convex, asymmetric payoff — full upside participation with the downside floored at the premium paid.

04
Sizing & cash

Uniform 1× — Cash Earns Fed Funds

Every signal-positive name gets the same one-unit premium outlay — no recovery scaling, no martingale. Premiums average just 1.3% of capital, so ~98.7% sits in cash earning the effective Fed Funds rate. A 4× premium-outlay variant quadruples exposure while ~95% of capital still stays in cash — the leverage is embedded in the options, nothing is borrowed.

Key Risks

◈

Premium Decay

Theta works against long options. In low-momentum, trending-sideways markets, premiums erode month after month with no payoff.

◇

Vol Regime Shifts

Periods of realised vol compression raise premium cost relative to payoff, reducing expected edge. Backtest before Sep 2020 is VIX-scaled but future regimes are unknown.

⬡

Momentum Crowding

Short-term momentum is a well-known factor. As capital crowds the trade the edge compresses. Signal decay is a permanent risk for any systematic momentum strategy.

◉

Execution Slippage

Up to ~95 concurrent options positions (median ~60). Bid-ask spreads and market impact are not modelled and would reduce real-world returns materially.
